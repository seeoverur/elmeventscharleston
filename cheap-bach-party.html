Bachelorette Party Budget Spreadsheet Charleston

Plan an amazing bachelorette party without breaking the bank! Our budget-friendly guide helps you experience the Holy City's charm, beaches, and entertainment while keeping costs under control.

Plan Your Celebration

Budget-Friendly Activities for Your Trip

Affordable Spa Options

Enjoy mani-pedis and salon services at budget-friendly spots. Group packages at local salons offer great rates for bachelorette parties.

Beach & Free Activities

Folly Beach offers free beach access, sunset watching, and the historic Folly Beach Pier. Pack a cooler and enjoy the ocean without spending.

BYOB Cooking Night

Skip expensive cooking classes and host your own Lowcountry cooking night at your rental. Find recipes online and cook together.

Budget Restaurants & Cafes for Your Getaway

  • Taco Boy - Affordable Mexican food and margaritas in Folly Beach. Great group atmosphere with reasonable prices.
  • Lost Dog Cafe - Casual breakfast and lunch spot in Folly Beach. Perfect for budget-friendly morning meals.
  • Sesame Burgers & Beer - Creative burgers at wallet-friendly prices.
  • Home Team BBQ - Delicious BBQ that won't break the bank. Sullivan's Island and West Ashley locations offer great value.
  • Jack of Cups Saloon - Budget-friendly bar food and drinks in a relaxed Park Circle setting.
  • Charleston Crab House - Affordable seafood with daily specials. Great views at reasonable prices.
  • Voodoo Tiki Bar & Lounge - Fun atmosphere with affordable tropical drinks and bar food in Mount Pleasant.
  • King Street Grille - Budget steaks and pasta in downtown without the premium prices.

Affordable Bars & Nightlife Options

  • The Washout (Folly Beach) - Beach bar with affordable drinks, live music, and oceanfront views.
  • Coleman Public House - Park Circle dive bar with cheap drinks and a fun local crowd.
  • Pour House (Folly Beach) - Budget-friendly beach bar with live music and no cover charge most nights.
  • Rec Room - Affordable craft beers and creative bar food. Great for groups on a budget.
  • Burns Alley Tavern - Downtown dive bar with cheap drinks and karaoke nights.
  • AC's Bar & Grill - Local hangout with affordable drinks and a relaxed atmosphere.
  • Zia Taqueria - Taco Tuesday specials and affordable margaritas in Park Circle.
  • The Royal American - Live music venue with reasonable drink prices and free shows many nights.

Entertainment That Fits Your Budget

  • Michael After Dark - Professional entertainment for your bachelorette party! Split the cost among your group for an unforgettable experience. Perfect for vacation rentals or hotel suites. THE show that delivers excitement without draining your budget when everyone chips in!

Ready to Plan Your Budget-Friendly Trip?

The city offers amazing experiences at every price point. From free beach days to affordable dining and split-cost entertainment—create unforgettable memories without the financial stress!
