Top Activities for Your Girls Trip Over 30

Spa & Pampering

Indulge in a luxurious spa day with massages, facials, and mani-pedis. The perfect way to relax and reconnect with your girlfriends.

King Street Shopping

Explore King Street's upscale boutiques, local designers, and curated shops. From home décor to fashion, find treasures you actually want.

Lowcountry Cooking Class

Master authentic cuisine with a hands-on cooking class featuring shrimp and grits, she-crab soup, and coastal classics.

Best Restaurants & Cafes for Your Girls Getaway

  • Husk Restaurant - Upscale Southern cuisine in a historic Victorian mansion. Perfect for a special celebratory dinner with sophisticated flavors.
  • The Darling Oyster Bar - Fresh oysters, seafood towers, and coastal vibes in a chic downtown setting with excellent wine pairings.
  • Halls Chophouse - Premium steaks and live music make this an elegant choice for a memorable group dinner.
  • Fleet Landing Restaurant - Waterfront dining with stunning harbor views and fresh seafood specialties in a relaxed atmosphere.
  • Poogan's Porch - Classic Lowcountry dishes in a charming historic house with genuine Southern hospitality.
  • Sweet Palm Coffee - Quick grab-and-go spot for artisan coffee and breakfast treats to fuel your adventures.
  • Leon's Oyster Shop - Casual vibes with fried chicken, oysters, and a fun rooftop bar perfect for afternoon drinks.
  • The Ordinary - Seafood hall and oyster bar serving exceptional raw bar selections and coastal fare in a beautiful space.

Hottest Bars & Nightlife for Your Weekend Escape

  • Prohibition - Upscale speakeasy with expertly crafted cocktails and a sophisticated atmosphere on King Street.
  • The Rooftop Bar at The Vendue - Stunning rooftop views, signature cocktails, and the perfect sunset gathering spot.
  • Mynt Bar - High-energy nightclub with DJ music and dancing for those nights you're ready to let loose.
  • The Cocktail Club - Creative craft cocktails in an intimate speakeasy-style setting with knowledgeable bartenders.
  • Pavilion Bar - Outdoor bar at The Market Pavilion Hotel with harbor views and live music in an elegant setting.
  • Stars Rooftop & Grill Room - Trendy rooftop lounge with panoramic city views and handcrafted drinks.
  • The Griffin - British pub atmosphere with excellent beer selection and late-night eats in a cozy environment.
  • Republic Garden & Lounge - Outdoor garden bar perfect for groups, featuring live music and creative cocktails.

Must-See Entertainment for Your Weekend

  • Michael After Dark - The ultimate entertainment experience for your girls trip over 30! Professional, unforgettable performances that bring the fun without the college crowd energy.
