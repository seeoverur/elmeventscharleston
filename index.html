Top Activities for Your Bride Era Celebration

Spa & Pampering

Indulge in a luxurious spa day with massages, facials, and mani-pedis. The perfect way to relax before the festivities begin.

King Street Shopping

Shop till you drop on King Street, featuring boutiques, local designers, and everything you need for your lingerie shower and beyond.

Lowcountry Cooking Class

Learn to cook authentic Charleston cuisine with a hands-on cooking class featuring shrimp and grits, she-crab soup, and more.

Best Restaurants & Cafes for Your Girls Getaway

  • Husk Restaurant - Upscale Southern cuisine in a historic Victorian mansion. Perfect for a special celebratory dinner.
  • The Darling Oyster Bar - Fresh oysters, seafood towers, and coastal vibes in a chic downtown setting.
  • Halls Chophouse - Premium steaks and live music make this an elegant choice for the bride's crew.
  • Fleet Landing Restaurant - Waterfront dining with stunning harbor views and fresh seafood specialties.
  • Poogan's Porch - Classic Lowcountry dishes in a charming historic house with Southern hospitality.
  • Sweet Palm Coffee - Quick grab-and-go spot for amazing coffee and breakfast treats to fuel your adventures.
  • Leon's Oyster Shop - Casual vibes with fried chicken, oysters, and a fun rooftop bar experience.
  • The Ordinary - Seafood hall and oyster bar serving exceptional raw bar selections and coastal fare.

Hottest Bars & Nightlife for Your Main Character Weekend

  • Prohibition - Upscale speakeasy with craft cocktails and a sophisticated atmosphere on King Street.
  • The Rooftop Bar at The Vendue - Stunning rooftop views, signature cocktails, and the perfect sunset spot.
  • Mynt Bar - High-energy nightclub with DJ music and dancing for those ready to party all night.
  • The Cocktail Club - Creative craft cocktails in an intimate speakeasy-style setting.
  • Pavilion Bar - Outdoor bar at The Market Pavilion Hotel with harbor views and live music.
  • Stars Rooftop & Grill Room - Trendy rooftop lounge with panoramic city views and handcrafted drinks.
  • The Griffin - British pub atmosphere with excellent beer selection and late-night eats.
  • Republic Garden & Lounge - Outdoor garden bar perfect for groups, featuring live music and creative cocktails.
