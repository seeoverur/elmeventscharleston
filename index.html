Things To Do In Charleston, SC For A Girls Trip

A guide for your girls weekend, main character weekend, or the ultimate getaway before the big day — historic charm by afternoon, harbor breeze by night.

Top Activities

Fine Dining

Indulge in a luxurious meal — the perfect way to energize before the festivities begin.

King Street Crawl

Walk it off on King Street, past boutiques, local eateries, and no shortage of bars.

Find A Local Show

Catch authentic Charleston comedy, live music, or a late-night set.

Best Restaurants & Cafes for Your Girls Getaway

  • Husk Restaurant

    Upscale Southern cuisine in a historic Victorian mansion — perfect for a celebratory dinner.

  • The Darling Oyster Bar

    Fresh oysters, seafood towers, and coastal vibes in a chic downtown setting.

  • Halls Chophouse

    Premium steaks and live music make this an elegant choice for the crew.

  • Fleet Landing Restaurant

    Waterfront dining with stunning harbor views and fresh seafood specialties.

  • Poogan's Porch

    Classic Lowcountry dishes in a charming historic house with Southern hospitality.

  • Sweet Palm Coffee

    Quick grab-and-go spot for great coffee and breakfast treats to fuel the day.

  • Leon's Oyster Shop

    Casual vibes with fried chicken, oysters, and a fun rooftop bar.

  • The Ordinary

    Seafood hall and oyster bar serving exceptional raw bar selections.

  • The Chubby Fish

    Chef-driven seafood spot on Rutledge with a tight, ever-changing menu of the day's catch.

  • Oak Steakhouse

    Elegant downtown steakhouse in a historic bank building, known for dry-aged cuts.

  • Hyman's Seafood

    Charleston institution serving classic Lowcountry seafood in a lively, packed room.

Hottest Bars & Nightlife for Your Main Character Weekend

  • Prohibition

    Upscale speakeasy with craft cocktails and a sophisticated atmosphere on King Street.

  • The Rooftop Bar at The Vendue

    Stunning rooftop views, signature cocktails, and the perfect sunset spot.

  • Mynt Bar

    High-energy nightclub with DJ music and dancing for those ready to go all night.

  • The Cocktail Club

    Creative craft cocktails in an intimate speakeasy-style setting.

  • Pavilion Bar

    Outdoor bar at The Market Pavilion Hotel with harbor views and live music.

  • Stars Rooftop & Grill Room

    Trendy rooftop lounge with panoramic city views and handcrafted drinks.

  • The Griffin

    British pub atmosphere with an excellent beer selection and late-night eats.

  • Republic Garden & Lounge

    Outdoor garden bar perfect for groups, with live music and creative cocktails.

  • OnAir Live

    Live music venue and bar bringing high-energy shows and a fun crowd.

  • Uptown Social

    Rooftop bar and lounge with skyline views, great for a lively group toast.

  • Trio

    Restaurant, bar, and rooftop combo on King Street with a lively late-night scene.

  • Frontier Lounge

    Laid-back neighborhood bar with a quirky vibe and a strong drink menu.

  • Shem Creek Bars

    A strip of waterfront bars in Mount Pleasant, perfect for sunset drinks and dockside hangs.

  • Honky Tonk Ladson

    Country bar with line dancing, live music, and a rowdy good time for the crew.

Ready to Plan Your Fling Before the Ring?

Charleston offers everything you need for an epic girls weekend — beach days, nightlife, fine dining, and unforgettable entertainment. Make your main character weekend!
