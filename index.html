Things To Do In Charleston With The Girls: A Girls Getaway Guide

Planning a girls weekend getaway in Charleston, SC? Start with a dinner worth dressing up for, follow it with rooftop drinks over the harbor, and finish the night with live music or a fling before the ring show. Here is how to fill a main character weekend downtown.

Charleston Girls Weekend Ideas

Three easy ways to anchor your girls trip before the night out begins.

Fine Dining For The Girls

Book one long, luxurious dinner early in the trip. It sets the tone and gives everyone the energy for the nights ahead.

King Street Bar Crawl

Walk it off along King Street, where boutiques, local eateries, and bars sit close enough to hop between all afternoon and evening.

Live Music And Late-Night Shows

Catch Charleston comedy, a live band, or a late-night set. Entertainment is what turns a good girls trip into a story you tell for years.

Charleston Girls Trip Restaurants: Seafood, Steakhouses & Southern Dinners

Where to eat and drink on a girls weekend, sorted by the kind of dinner you are in the mood for.

Oyster Bars & Seafood Dinners

  • The Darling Oyster Bar

    Fresh oysters, seafood towers, and coastal vibes in a chic downtown setting, ideal for a girls trip dinner that starts with a toast.

  • The Ordinary

    Seafood hall and oyster bar serving exceptional raw bar selections for a group that likes to share.

  • The Chubby Fish

    Chef-driven seafood spot on Rutledge with a tight, ever-changing menu built around the day's catch.

  • Fleet Landing Restaurant

    Waterfront dining with stunning harbor views and fresh seafood specialties.

  • Hyman's Seafood

    A Charleston institution serving classic Lowcountry seafood in a lively, packed room.

Steakhouse & Fine Dining

  • Halls Chophouse

    Premium steaks and live music make this an elegant steakhouse dinner for the crew.

  • Oak Steakhouse

    Elegant downtown steakhouse in a historic bank building, known for dry-aged cuts.

  • Husk Restaurant

    Upscale Southern cuisine in a historic Victorian mansion, perfect for a celebratory girls weekend dinner.

Southern Classics & Casual Bites

  • Poogan's Porch

    Classic Lowcountry dishes in a charming historic house with true Southern hospitality.

  • Leon's Oyster Shop

    Casual vibes with fried chicken, oysters, and a fun rooftop bar.

  • Sweet Palm Coffee

    A quick grab-and-go spot for great coffee and breakfast treats to fuel the morning after.

Charleston Girls Trip Entertainment: A Fling Before The Ring

Michael At Your Place

The ultimate entertainment experience for your fling before the ring in Charleston, SC. Professional, unforgettable performances that give your celebration the main character moment it deserves. Perfect for lingerie shower ideas, hotel suite parties, or private venue celebrations. This is the show that will have everyone talking long after the weekend.

Charleston Girls Night Out: Rooftop Bars, Speakeasies & Live Music

The hottest bars and nightlife for your main character weekend, from harbor-view rooftops to dance floors that stay open late.

Rooftop Bars With Harbor & Skyline Views

  • The Rooftop Bar at The Vendue

    Stunning rooftop views over the harbor, signature cocktails, and the perfect sunset spot.

  • Stars Rooftop & Grill Room

    Trendy rooftop lounge with panoramic city views and handcrafted drinks.

  • Uptown Social

    Rooftop bar and lounge with skyline views, great for a lively group toast.

  • Pavilion Bar

    Rooftop bar at The Market Pavilion Hotel with harbor views and live music.

  • Trio

    Restaurant, bar, and rooftop combo on King Street with a lively late-night scene.

Speakeasies & Craft Cocktail Bars

  • Prohibition

    Upscale speakeasy with craft cocktails and a sophisticated atmosphere on King Street.

  • The Cocktail Club

    Creative craft cocktails in an intimate speakeasy-style setting.

  • The Griffin

    British pub atmosphere with an excellent beer selection and late-night eats.

  • Frontier Lounge

    Laid-back neighborhood bar with a quirky vibe and a strong drink menu.

Live Music, Dancing & Country Bars

  • Republic Garden & Lounge

    Outdoor garden bar perfect for groups, with live music and creative cocktails.

  • OnAir Live

    Live music venue and bar bringing high-energy shows and a fun crowd.

  • Mynt Bar

    High-energy nightclub with DJ music and dancing for those ready to go all night.

  • Honky Tonk Ladson

    Country bar with line dancing, live music, and a rowdy good time for the crew.

Sunset Drinks Over The Water

  • Shem Creek Bars

    A strip of waterfront bars in Mount Pleasant, perfect for sunset drinks and dockside hangs.

A Charleston Girls Weekend Idea, Night By Night

Mix and match the spots above into a simple three-day flow for your girls getaway.

Friday: Dinner & Rooftop

Arrive, drop the bags, and start with oysters at The Darling Oyster Bar or The Ordinary. End the night with drinks at The Rooftop Bar at The Vendue.

Saturday: King Street & The Show

Spend the afternoon on a King Street bar crawl, book a steakhouse dinner at Halls Chophouse or Oak Steakhouse, then celebrate your fling before the ring with a private show.

Sunday: Coffee & Sunset Drinks

Slow down with breakfast treats from Sweet Palm Coffee, then head across the bridge for sunset drinks on Shem Creek.

Ready To Plan Your Fling Before The Ring?

Charleston has everything a girls weekend getaway needs: fine dining, rooftop bars, live music, and unforgettable entertainment. Make it your main character weekend.
