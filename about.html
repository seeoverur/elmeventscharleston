About Elm Events Charleston

Welcome to Elm Events Charleston, where unforgettable celebrations come to life in the heart of the Lowcountry. We are your dedicated event planning partner, specializing in creating extraordinary experiences that capture the unique charm and vibrant energy of Charleston, South Carolina.

From intimate gatherings to large-scale celebrations, we bring creativity, professionalism, and local expertise to every event we plan. Our deep knowledge of Charleston's venues, vendors, and hidden gems allows us to craft experiences that are authentically Charleston while being uniquely yours.

Our Mission

To create seamless, memorable celebrations that exceed expectations and showcase the best of Charleston's hospitality, culture, and charm. We believe every event should be a reflection of your vision, executed flawlessly, and enjoyed without stress.

What We Offer

At Elm Events Charleston, we specialize in a wide range of celebrations and events throughout the Charleston area, including Mount Pleasant, Isle of Palms, Folly Beach, and downtown Charleston. Our services include:

  • Pre-wedding celebrations and girls getaways
  • Birthday parties and milestone celebrations
  • Corporate events and team gatherings
  • Private parties and special occasions
  • Event coordination and vendor management
  • Venue selection and logistics planning
  • Entertainment booking and activity planning
  • Custom event experiences tailored to your vision
